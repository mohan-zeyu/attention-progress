## Must history stay as separate records?

Keep a growing collection of records, or continually update an aggregate state.

![History can remain separate records or update a bounded aggregate state. At fixed model dimensions, record count grows but decode-state dimensions stay fixed.](../figures/visual/00-organizations.svg)

> At fixed model size, recurrent decode state has fixed dimensions.

## What can we make smaller?

Within the record route, change sharing, record width or record count.

![Three operations within addressable records: sharing heads, shrinking each record and pooling positions. These are operations, with named designs shown underneath.](../figures/visual/00-choices.svg)

> GQA and MLA name designs. Sequence compression names an operation; recurrent attention names a family.
